Group-1

## Dataset Title

Women Civil Liberties Index (Canada, 1960-2024).

## Dataset Source

https://data360.worldbank.org/en/indicator/VDEM_CORE_V2X_GENCL?view=trend&country=CAN&minYear=1960

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Dataset Description

This dataset contains information on the amount of civil liberties experienced by women over time in different countries. In this specific case, this subset looks at the years of 1960-2024, focusing on Canada. It includes variables related to the rights and freedoms of women, looking heavily on justice accessibility, political/civil participation, and freedom of movement. The dataset was collected to analyze the differences, changes, and evolutions in women's civil liberties over a specific timeframe, while comparing and contrasting countries across the world.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Who Collects and Verifies the Data?

The data is collected and verified by the V-Dem (Varieties of Democracy Institute), based at the University of Gothenburg in Gothenburg, Sweden. Academic researchers, political & global experts, & statistical modelers are contributors, while the project relies heavily on international coders to apply different statistical models to constantly improve and refine the data.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## How Frequently is the Data Updated?

The dataset is updated regularly. The newest version includes data up to 2024, with a new dataset release happening every year.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Data Collection Methods

The methods used in the collection of this data are:
-   Historical analyses
-   Surveying global specialists
-   Using statistical modeling to visually represent findings

These methods help ensure accuract in the dollected data, while removing political biases.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Raw Data or Aggregated Data?

The dataset is representative of aggregated data. Global experts give ratings based on several measures which are then combined using statistical models which then produce the index.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Key Variables in the Dataset

Variables in the dataset include:
-   Country
-   Year
-   Women civil liberties index score
-   Measures related to women's rights (justice accessibility, freedom of movement, etc.)

These variables are useful for our research question as it answers and highlights the following:
-   How have women's rights evolved over time?
-   Comparing the rate at which gender equality grew over periods of times in different countries
-   Whether civil liberties are currently in a decline, and if so, how much of a decline from the 1990s (end of the timeframe for our chosen project)

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## File Format

JSON / PDF / Wide Format / SDMX CSV / Data dictionary

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Notes

We notices some limitation about the dataset:
-   Index scores are not exact and show estimates
-   The dataset is large so filtering is necessary which is difficult when wanting to look at the differences between multiple countries
-   Because the data is based on expert responses and surveys, data may be slightly skewed based off of what the V-Dem’s definition of an "expert" is
