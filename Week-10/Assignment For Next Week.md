# Assignment For Week 11 --- Collect and Evaluate a Dataset

## Overview

For this assignment, your group will:

1.  Find a dataset from a public database\
2.  Evaluate the quality of the dataset\
3.  Upload the dataset to the course GitHub repository\
4.  Document the dataset using a `README.md` file

This assignment will help you begin building the dataset you will use
for your **final project**.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Step 1 --- Collect Your Data

Your group must download a **real dataset from a public database**.

## Possible Data Sources

You may use one of the following types of databases:

-   Government open data portals\
-   Academic research databases\
-   Cultural heritage datasets\
-   Social media or digital culture datasets\
-   International organizations

### Example Sources

-   https://data.gc.ca\
-   https://data.un.org\
-   https://www.kaggle.com/datasets\
-   https://ourworldindata.org\
-   https://data.worldbank.org

### Requirements

Your dataset should:

-   Be **downloadable** (CSV, JSON, XLSX, TSV preferred)
-   Include **multiple variables**
-   Contain **enough data for analysis**
-   Be **related to your research question**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Step 2 --- Evaluate Dataset Quality

Before using the dataset, evaluate its **credibility and usefulness**.

Discuss the following questions with your group:

-   Who collects and verifies the data?\
    (Government agencies, researchers, organizations, or crowdsourced?)

-   How frequently is the data updated?\
    (Is it current or outdated?)

-   Does the source explain the **data collection methods**?\
    (Surveys, web scraping, sensors, AI-generated, etc.)

-   Does the source provide **raw microdata** or only **aggregated
    statistics**?

-   Does the dataset include the **key variables you need**?\
    (Examples: age, location, engagement data, timestamps, etc.)

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Step 3 --- Upload Your Dataset to GitHub

After downloading your dataset, upload it to the **course GitHub
repository**.

### Instructions

1.  Go to the course repository on GitHub.
2.  Switch to the branch: **Final-Projects**
3.  Create a new folder for your group using this format:

-   Group-1/README.md
-   Group-2/README.md
-   Group-3/README.md

4.  Upload your **raw dataset** into your group folder after it is created.

Accepted formats:

-   `.csv`
-   `.json`
-   `.xlsx`
-   `.tsv`

5.  After uploading the dataset:

-   Click the file in GitHub\
-   Make sure the data **displays correctly and is readable**

------------------------------------------------------------------------
